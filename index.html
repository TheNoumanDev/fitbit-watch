Fitbit Watch

Fitbit Watch is a personal, single-user tool. It reads its owner's own health data from the Google Health API (steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV and similar daily summaries), keeps a daily history on the owner's computer, and builds a private dashboard from it.

It is not a public service: no one else can sign up, and it never asks anyone other than its owner for access.
